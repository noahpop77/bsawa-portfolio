Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

This portfolio is a personal website operated by Brian Sawa. It does not use analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, or third-party cookies.

What I collect

The only information collected is what you choose to send through the contact form: your name, email address, and message. That information is used solely to reply to you.

How messages are delivered

The contact form is processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form-to-email service. Your submission is transmitted to FormSubmit and forwarded to my email inbox. Please review FormSubmit's own privacy practices before submitting sensitive information.

If no form endpoint is configured, the form opens a draft in your own email client instead, and no data is sent to this website.

Third-party content

  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces used on this site and may log the request.
  • YouTube embeds are loaded only when you open a video preview.
  • Links to LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, and YouTube are governed by those platforms' policies.

Retention

Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain a record of the conversation. You can ask me to delete your message at any time.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of any message you have sent by emailing bsawasec@gmail.com.
